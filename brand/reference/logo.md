# Logo

The logo has two parts: the symbol (a tyre ring in ink with 24 slanted tread grooves, and an olive-khaki tick breaking out through its top right) and the wordmark (TREADMARK in Martel Sans ExtraBold capitals, tracked +7%, outlined). Never retype the wordmark; always use the files.

## Construction

| Element | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Ring stroke | Equal to the letter stem (23.2% of cap height) |
| Tick stroke | Equal to the stem: ring, tick and letters share one weight (the tread already takes some weight out of the ring) |
| Tick angle | 49.4°, parallel to the arm of the K |
| Ring size | 1.75 cap heights, centred on the capitals |
| Tick position | Lowered so the whole symbol's centre of mass sits on the ring's centre |
| Break | Half a stem of clear space either side of the tick |
| Tread | 24 slanted grooves cut in from the outer edge, turned so the grooves either side of the break sit the same distance from it |
| Symbol to word | 0.40 cap height |

## Versions

| Version | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| Horizontal lockup | Default. Website, report header, documents, decks. |
| Small lockup | The same, with a plain ring (no tread), for anything where the symbol is under 64px or 18 mm tall: app header, email signature, social posts seen on a phone. |
| Tagline lockup | With "Every car, properly checked." under the wordmark, for marketing. |
| Hindi lockup | With "हर गाड़ी की पक्की जाँच" under the wordmark, for Hindi marketing. The wordmark stays Latin. |
| Stacked lockup | Square spaces: splash screen, stickers, the back of a business card. |
| Symbol | App icon, profile pictures, watermark on report photos. Use the small symbol under 64px. |
| Certificate seal | Reports, certificates and windscreen stickers only. Olive, or ink for black-and-white print. |

Colourways: `color` (ink ring and word, olive tick, on light grounds), `reverse` (white ring and word, olive-light tick, on `brand-night`), `olive` (all-olive symbol, ink word: the single-ink stamp), `mono-ink` and `mono-white`.

## Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo equal to one cap height of the wordmark. Nothing enters it, including photo edges and the edge of the screen or page.

## Minimum size

- Horizontal lockup: 120px wide on screen, 35 mm in print.
- Small lockup: 72px wide, 20 mm in print. Below that, use the small symbol alone.
- Favicons and app icons at 48px and below use the plain ring automatically.

## Colour rules

- On light grounds use `color`; on `brand-night` or any dark photo use `reverse`.
- Black-and-white printing and photocopies: always `mono-ink`. Olive and ink are 2.8:1 apart, better than before but still faint in greyscale.
- Never put the olive tick on a dark ground; that is what the olive-light `reverse` is for.
- On busy photos, set the logo on a solid paper or night panel, or move it off the photo.

## Do not

- Recolour the ring or tick separately, swap their colours, or put sandstone in the logo.
- Stretch, skew, rotate, outline, add a shadow or glow, or put the logo in a box.
- Re-space the letters, rebuild the lockup with different spacing, or put the symbol on the right.
- Notch the ring like a gear, or straighten the tread grooves.
- Write the name in Devanagari. "ट्रेडमार्क" reads as the Hindi word for "trademark", so Hindi materials keep the Latin wordmark with the Hindi tagline.

## App icon

Olive Khaki tile, the symbol in paper. Rounded corners are added by the platform; the `icon-*.png` "any" files carry a 22.5% corner radius for places that do not round. Maskable and Play Store icons are full-bleed, with the symbol inside the safe zone. The monochrome icon is for Android themed icons.
