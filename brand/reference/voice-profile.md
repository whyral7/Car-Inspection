# Treadmark voice profile

Treadmark writes like an inspector signing a report: the reading first, then what it means, then what to do.

## Rhythm and claims
- Short declarative sentences, 6 to 14 words. One fact per sentence. Findings come as fact, then action.
- Numbers over adjectives: "Tread 2.1 mm", never "tyres are worn".
- Claim only what was inspected: "No structural repair found", never "accident-free".
- Hedge only with the method's real limit: "Scan again after 100 km of driving."
- Describe the car, never the person: "The odometer reading doesn't match the service record."
- Use the real checkpoint count from the checklist config. Never hard-code a number like 220.

## Banned
- Exclamation marks; emoji in the app and reports.
- "Accident-free", "100% guaranteed", "best in India", "certified" when no seal was issued.
- Fear-selling ("Don't get cheated"), blaming sellers or dealers, unsourced social proof ("trusted by thousands").
- Tech filler: "AI-powered", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "revolutionary".
- Rhetorical or hook questions; questions only in forms.

## Form
- Sentence case everywhere, buttons included. Capitals only for the wordmark, overline labels, plates and report IDs.
- One call to action, verb first, saying what happens: "Download report", "Book an inspection", "Verify this certificate".
- "You" for the reader; "we" only when Treadmark takes responsibility.
- Formats: ₹4,85,000 · ₹5.60–5.85 lakh · 48,320 km · 9 Nov 2026 · 2.1 mm · 118 µm.
- Scores: 8.4/10 with a grade word. No stars, no percentages.
- Hindi: written natively, formal आप, everyday words (गाड़ी, डिक्की, स्टेपनी; not वाहन). Acronyms, units and brand names stay in Latin script (RC, PUC, OTP, PDF, AI, km, WhatsApp, Treadmark); loanwords people say in Hindi are written in Devanagari (रिपोर्ट, फ़ोटो, क्रेडिट).
- Tagline: "Every car, properly checked." · हर गाड़ी की पक्की जाँच

## Examples
| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Tyres are badly worn! | Front left tread 2.1 mm. Replace within 3,000 km. |
| This car is accident-free. | No structural repair found. Chassis and engine numbers match the RC. |
| The seller has hidden a repaint. | Two panels repainted: 118 µm against a 95 µm average. Ask for the repair bill. |
| Oops! Something went wrong. | The photo didn't upload. Check your signal and try again. |
| Our AI-powered platform delivers seamless inspections. | An inspector checks every point. Every finding comes with its photo and reading. |
